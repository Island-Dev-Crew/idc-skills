# SEATS — the fleet roles

- **executor** — exercises an island across real challenge cases in a sandbox; reports what
  happened, not what should happen.
- **judge** — adversarial; scores by the defect-tied rubric. A limitation of an external system
  that the island *correctly discloses* is not a defect — honest disclosure is the fix.
- **fixer** — applies the smallest real fix; never bloats (a clause, not a paragraph); preserves
  the enforced-vs-advisory voice and the island's one concern.
- **reviewer** — a different model family at an exact head (`cross-family-review`); the author
  never reviews their own work; the verdict names its seats and voids the moment the head moves.
- **builder + blind critic** — the `gauntlet-loop` pair: builders fan out, each shadowed by a
  blind critic scoring the artifact against a *falsifiable* bar. For wow-grade builds, not gates.
